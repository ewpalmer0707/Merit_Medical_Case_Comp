## 1. Where the three agree (the spine)
1. **Sort every supplier first, using a rule.** Elijah's tiers, Nolan's "assess all, then match support to the obstacle," and Cameron's three-track whiteboard are the same idea. The rule (criticality/switching difficulty × spend × country risk × readiness) is the template that scales to 1,030.
2. **Reuse what exists before asking for anything new.** Anchors share existing ratings; nobody fills out a duplicate questionnaire.
3. **Help is targeted, not blanket.** Intensive support goes only to critical suppliers with a real barrier.
4. **Merit doesn't over-claim.** Report coverage and gaps honestly; label estimates.
