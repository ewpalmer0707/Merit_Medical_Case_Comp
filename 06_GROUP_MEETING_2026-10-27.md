## Work that supplier support would actually perform

The group described five practical steps:

1. Gather documents and data the supplier already holds.
2. Determine what those records establish and which requested information is missing.
3. Establish how genuinely missing information will be collected.
4. Help the supplier complete and submit the questionnaire with supporting evidence.
5. Leave a repeatable process with an owner so the supplier can maintain records and report again.

An important distinction for the eventual proposal: finding an existing document is different from establishing a measurement process that does not yet exist. An AI tool or consultant cannot recover measurements that were never recorded merely by reading more files.

### 2. A completed EcoVadis assessment does not establish every desired outcome

EcoVadis assesses sustainability management using supporting evidence. Its rating is useful, but the proposal must distinguish questionnaire completion, evidence quality, supplier emissions data, changes in actual practices, and supply continuity. An assessment score alone is not automatically a complete or independently assured carbon inventory.

Sources:
- [EcoVadis Sustainability Ratings](https://ecovadis.com/solutions/ratings/)
- [EcoVadis scoring principles](https://resources.ecovadis.com/ecovadis-solution-materials/csr-rating-methodology-scoring-principles)

### 3. AI-assisted completion must stay grounded in actual practices

EcoVadis says answers are credited only when properly supported. It does not accept documentation created solely to satisfy the questionnaire or copied policies/actions without reasonable evidence of implementation. Tools can help locate and organize evidence; a polished answer cannot substitute for missing records or practices.

Source: [Understanding supporting documents](https://support.ecovadis.com/hc/en-us/articles/210460307-Understanding-supporting-documents).

### 4. Chinese-language availability already exists

EcoVadis Sustainability Ratings supports simplified Chinese, including registration, questionnaire, platform, and scorecard. Customer Care supports Chinese. The team can still investigate practical support needs, but should not describe translating the basic questionnaire into Chinese as a missing EcoVadis feature.

Source: [Languages supported by EcoVadis](https://support.ecovadis.com/hc/en-us/articles/14986355109522-What-are-the-languages-supported-by-EcoVadis).
