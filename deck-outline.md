## 1 · Title + hook · Elijah · 0:30
- **Headline:** Merit Supplier Ladder
- **Subhead:** Credible data from the supply chain partners patients can't lose
- **On-slide:** T_23 · team names
- **Visual:** a full-bleed photo of a syringe/custom procedure kit, darkened, with the title over it.
- **Say:**
  - In 2024 a Chinese syringe maker, whose syringes reached Merit's custom kits through one of Merit's suppliers, got an FDA warning letter. Merit recalled the kits, and Cardinal Health's kits that contained them were recalled too.
  - It wasn't an emissions problem; it was a *visibility* problem. The same blind spot hides labor and emissions risk.
  - "Today we'll show how Merit catches the next one earlier."
- **Source:** FDA warning letter Mar 2024; FDA recall Z-database Jun 2024

## 2 · The core problem · Elijah · 1:00
- **Headline:** Merit asks every supplier the same question, so the ones that matter most can't answer it
- **On-slide:** two columns under the headline
  - **Data gap in the wrong place:** 1,030 suppliers · 42 countries · top 78 = 65% of spend · requalifying = **9–12 months**
  - **Story ahead of the numbers:** Scope 3 unmeasured · Scope 1+2 **−2%** vs a **−40%** goal · no EcoVadis coverage published
  - Bottom strip, "Why now": new CEO · EcoVadis new in 2025 · EU vague-claims ban Sept 2026 · NHS Scope 1–3 plan Apr 2027
- **Visual:** left, a supplier dot map with Mexico/China dots highlighted amber; right, a thermometer bar showing −2% of −40%.
- **Say:**
  - Big suppliers answered fast. Small single-plant makers in Mexico and China, the ones we can't replace, didn't.
  - Merit's own 2025 report names Scope 3 from China and Mexico as a top risk.
  - It's fixable now because the EcoVadis rollout is one year old.
- **Source:** Case Exh. 2; Merit 2025 Sustainability Report; EU Directive 2024/825; NHS Net Zero Supplier Roadmap

## 3 · Why the defaults fail · Elijah · 0:40
- **Headline:** Neither default fixes the blind spot without breaking something
- **On-slide:** 3-column table: **A · Mandate everyone** · **B · Top 78 first** · **Ladder** (highlighted). Rows: spend coverage · risk coverage · supplier strain · HEART fit. Use ✓/✗/~ icons, not sentences.
- **Visual:** the table with the Ladder column in teal, A and B in grey.
- **Say:**
  - A gets forms, not data: unverified yes/no surveys average 89%, with 40% perfect scores. It also pushes suppliers out.
  - B is cheap, but it leaves the critical Mexico/China makers dark for years.
  - We keep B's speed and fix A's blind spot. Hand off to Nolan.
- **Source:** Case Exh. 5; `research/analogues-buyer-programs.md`

## 4 · The Ladder · Nolan · 1:00
- **Headline:** Sort every supplier with one rule, then help each one climb
- **On-slide:**
  - Left, **Sort:** Switching difficulty × Spend × Country risk × Readiness → 3 tiers
    - Tier 1 · Anchors (~65) · share an existing EcoVadis score, min 45
    - Tier 2 · Critical & stuck (~30–50) · climb with support
    - Tier 3 · Long tail (~900) · short form only
  - Right, **Climb:** 0 Estimate → 1 Reported → 2 Traceable → 3 Rated
- **Visual:** left, the Exhibit 3 power×readiness grid with the tiers overlaid; right, a large 4-rung ladder with one line under each rung. Tag "est." on the counts.
- **Say:**
  - Two tracks at once: lock in the easy 65% of spend in month 1, and put the effort into the critical few.
  - The rule is the deliverable. It scales to all 1,030.
  - Rung 0 is Merit's own spend-based estimate, labeled as one. Suppliers replace it with real records.
  - Two asks, never confused: the rating covers labor and ethics; emissions data feeds Scope 3.
- **Source:** Case Exh. 3; GHG Protocol Scope 3 Cat. 1 guidance; EcoVadis Vitals guide

## 5 · What partners get · Nolan · 0:50
- **Headline:** We ask what's blocking each supplier, then remove that barrier
- **On-slide:**
  - Top, a 4-row "barrier → help" table:
    - Reports exist → share them
    - Format unclear → bilingual template
    - Calculations hard → specialist hours
    - Big gaps → named Merit owner + visit
  - Bottom, 4 icons: **Merit pays the assessment** · **One scorecard, all customers** · **Earlier payment (NAFIN)** · **40-hour reform help**
- **Visual:** the table on top, an icon row below. The ladder icon sits on rung 1–2.
- **Say:**
  - It starts with a 5-question readiness request, and we follow up on silence before assuming "can't."
  - Suppliers keep ownership of their data; we don't fill forms for them.
  - Financial incentives made suppliers 52% more likely to cut emissions than training alone (CDP/HSBC).
- **Source:** CDP/HSBC 2024; WRAP 2023 audit survey; NAFIN Cadenas Productivas
