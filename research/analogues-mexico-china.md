# Analogues: Mexico and China small-supplier sustainability programs

Research for the Merit Medical supplier-sustainability case (BYU case competition). Scope: what has actually worked, and what has backfired, when buyers and institutions try to bring small, single-plant manufacturers in Mexico and China up to ESG/EcoVadis-type expectations. Compiled 2026-10-24 from public web sources only. Every fact carries a URL. Items marked [UNVERIFIED] came from a secondary source we could not confirm against the primary; [NOT FOUND] means we looked and found nothing public.

Caveat on source quality: a large share of the Mexico material is from press releases, trade press (Mexico Business News), and economic-development promotional sites (Tijuana EDC). Treat their numbers as "claimed by the organization" unless a government or multilateral document is cited.

## 2. Area 1: Mexico

### 2.1 IFC / INA / Secretaría de Economía Programa de Desarrollo de Proveedores (PDP)

What it is. Launched by INA (national auto-parts association) and IFC in January 2025 under Plan México; the three-way MoU with the Secretaría de Economía was signed 4 August 2025. It is a demand-driven model: anchor OEMs/Tier-1s state what they need, SMEs register, get a World Bank capabilities diagnostic (technical, operational, commercial gaps), and the highest-potential firms get training, mentoring, consulting, and coaching toward IATF 16949 / ISO 9001.
- 16 anchor firms, 35 supply-requirement areas, ~400 SMEs registered in phase 1. https://www.ifc.org/es/pressroom/2025/ifc-ina-y-la-secretar-a-de-econom-a-anuncian-nueva-fase-del-programa-de-desarrollo
- H2 2025: at least 55 SMEs trained; 40-50 more in 2026; >100 total in pilot. Same URL.
- Demo Day (Mar 2026): 55 companies trained, up to 20 received specialized consulting toward global certifications; IFC plans 50 more SMEs by H2 2026. https://mexicobusiness.news/automotive/news/ina-ifc-boost-auto-smes-under-plan-mexico-program
- Cost to SMEs: none. "El PDP no representa ningún costo para las empresas participantes." https://www.ifc.org/es/pressroom/2025/ifc-ina-y-la-secretar-a-de-econom-a-anuncian-nueva-fase-del-programa-de-desarrollo
- Finance: NAFIN, Bancomext and other banks facilitate credit; amounts not disclosed. Same URL.
- Anchor interest reported: GM, VW, Ford, Stellantis, BMW, Nissan, Mazda. https://mexicobusiness.news/automotive/news/supplier-development-auto-sector-commitment-smes
- ESG content: essentially none. INA's quote mentions a "más innovadora, digital y sostenible" industry; no ESG metrics or EcoVadis-type component. The press release says the model "could be replicated in other strategic sectors," which is the opening for a medical-device chapter.

Relevance to Merit: this is the closest existing Mexican vehicle for "anchor-firm-led, free-to-SME, diagnostic-then-coach" supplier development, and it already has IFC, SE, NAFIN and Bancomext plumbing. It is auto-only today.

### 2.8 Mexican ESG and emissions regulation touching SMEs

RENE (Registro Nacional de Emisiones, LGCC regulation 2014):
- Threshold 25,000 tCO2e/yr direct + indirect, within listed sectors (energy, industry, transport, agriculture, waste, commerce/services). Reported via the Cédula de Operación Anual, 1 Mar - 30 Jun; third-party verification every 3 years (1 Jul - 30 Nov). https://www.aenor.com/web/mex/certificacion/empresas/medio-ambiente/registro-nacional-de-emisiones ; https://www.manglai.io/blog/rene-programa-gei-mexico ; https://dcycle.io/blog/rene-mexico-emissions-registry-guide/

## 5. Area 4: Burden evidence and what incentives move small suppliers

- WRAP survey (525 facilities, 32 countries, Aug-Sept 2023, apparel): 2022 audit counts: 30.3% had 1-2, 46.2% had 3-5, 16.0% had 6-10, 5.2% had 11-15, 2.3% had 16+. Lost-business rate not measured. https://doi.org/10.3390/su17178029 ; https://research.tudelft.nl/en/publications/data-requests-in-value-chains-the-effects-of-corporate-sustainabi/

### 5.2 What incentives actually move small suppliers (with numbers)

- CDP/HSBC 2024 (23,000+ disclosers, 343 buyers in CDP Supply Chain): financial incentives (preferential financing, favourable contract terms) -> suppliers 52% more likely to cut annual emissions than with training alone; buyer help on SBTs -> 2.6x more likely to set one; training/support -> 1.7x more likely to run climate risk assessment; joint low-carbon R&D -> 4.3x more likely to set SBT; collecting GHG data annually -> 1.7x more likely to verify Scope 1/2; suppliers' disclosure rate 71% with 2-10 requesting buyers vs 92% with 11+; buyers on average engage suppliers covering <50% of spend; engagement produced 43 MtCO2e reported savings; recommended rewards: contract extensions, first access to RFPs, recognition. https://cdn.cdp.net/cdp-production/cms/reports/documents/000/007/890/original/CDP_HSBC_Report_2024.pdf ; summary: https://cdpturkey.sabanciuniv.edu/en/content/supply-chain-report-2024-strengthening-chain-0
- IFC GTSF tiered pricing: Levi's (2014) and Puma (2016, with BNP Paribas): the higher the supplier's audit score, the lower its receivables discount rate. Puma year 1: >$100M financing, 15% of supplier base. Score-improvement outcomes [NOT FOUND]. https://www.levistrauss.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/IFC_LSCO_Tiered-GTSF.pdf ; https://supplychaindigital.com/procurement/puma-and-ifc-reward-suppliers-emerging-markets-environmental-and-social-performance ; https://www.ifc-worldwide.com/en/what-we-do/sector-expertise/financial-institutions/global-trade/global-trade-supplier-finance.html
- Walmart/HSBC/CDP: early payment priced by CDP score or SBT; 5,000+ suppliers [UNVERIFIED WEF figure]. https://initiatives.weforum.org/financing-transition-community/case-study-details/hsbc-x-walmart/aJYTG0000000ZsD4AU ; https://www.esgtoday.com/walmart-to-promote-incentivize-emissions-reductions-in-supply-chain-with-hsbc-backed-financing-program/
