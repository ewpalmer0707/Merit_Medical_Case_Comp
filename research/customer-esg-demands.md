# What Merit's Customers Actually Ask Suppliers on ESG / Supply-Chain Sustainability (2025–2026)

Compiled 2026-10-27 for the Merit Medical case competition (Oct 30, 2026). This builds on `outside-research.md` §3 ("GPO / hospital-customer ESG screening") and does not repeat it. Every figure has a URL. **[UNVERIFIED]** = from a search snippet, secondary source, or a page I could not open. **[NOT FOUND]** = searched, no public source. **[DATED]** = source is older than 2025 and current status was not confirmed.

**The short version.** US customers *ask* for data. The NHS *gates* bids on it. France now *scores* it. No US GPO publishes a numeric ESG weight in its RFPs. UK public tenders carry a fixed minimum of 10%, and from April 2027 large NHS contracts need a global Scope 1–3 carbon plan. Across all customer types, the same handful of items keeps coming up: Scope 1/2 (and increasingly 3) emissions, a public reduction target, chemicals of concern (PVC/DEHP), packaging, and modern-slavery due diligence.

## 2. Evidence that ESG wins or loses business

- **Gates are real in the UK, not yet in the US.** NHS medicines and NHS Supply Chain notices exclude bidders who lack an Evergreen submission or CRP (SPS FAQ and tender notices above). In the US, **no public case was found of a medtech supplier winning or losing a hospital or GPO contract on ESG [NOT FOUND]**. GPOs collect the data and give awards (Vizient–Stryker 2026, HealthTrust–Merck 2023) but do not publish weights.
- **L.E.K. (MedTech Strategist, June 2024):** "approximately 40% of hospitals have sustainability requirements and around 50% plan to implement them in the next three years". Sustainability is moving "from a nice-to-have to a must-have to compete" in EU tenders and with leading US systems. Some systems see a "green premium" as "advantageous or even necessary". https://www.lek.com/sites/default/files/PDFs/rethinking-environmental-sustainability.pdf (survey method not disclosed)
- **Staples 2025 State of Healthcare Procurement (n=150, vendor-sponsored):** "96% of organizations identifying [sustainability] as a medium or high priority", described as "directly shaping supplier selection and procurement policies." https://marketingassets.staples.com/m/54883a824832f2c0/original/2025-State-of-Healthcare-Procurement_Staples.pdf (soft measure: "medium or high")
- **Advisory Board 2026 State of Healthcare Procurement (>100 health-system supply-chain leaders):** reliability is the top partner-selection priority (36%), and sustainability is seen as most effective when it lines up with core supply-chain goals. https://go.beckershospitalreview.com/supplywp/2026-state-of-healthcare-procurement ; https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/supply-chain/5-trends-reshaping-healthcare-procurement-in-2026/ (403; snippet) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Hospital efforts persisted after the federal rollback:** CleanMed 2025 drew >800 attendees, flat with 2024. https://www.hpnonline.com/sourcing-logistics/article/55292741/the-sustainability-of-sustainability
- **Older:** a 2012 J&J study found 35% of hospitals had switched suppliers over green offerings; a 2014 Harris/J&J poll found 54% factored sustainability into purchasing. https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/future-impact-of-environmental-scorecards-hospital-global-purchasing-guidelines-revealed-in-new-report-at-2012-cleanmed-europe-conference ; https://csrwire.com/press-release/globally-hospitals-are-driving-toward-greener-purchasing-decisions-greater-than-50-increase-expected-in-next-two-years/ **[DATED]**
- **AHRMM 2025–26 survey on ESG in supplier selection: [NOT FOUND].**
- **EcoVadis in hospital procurement:** there is little direct evidence. Heraeus Medical used its rating for an Australian hospital-association inquiry and says "purchasing groups" have signalled they will look at ratings. https://www.heraeus.com/en/hme/about_heraeus_medical/press_heraeus_medical/medical_news_2023/ecovadis_rating.html. Peers publicize medals to the NHS, e.g. Canon Medical UK Gold: https://uk.medical.canon/canon-medical-awarded-ecovadis-gold-medal-and-ranks-among-top-5-globally/. **No US GPO or health system requires an EcoVadis score [NOT FOUND].**
- **Merit's own customer-facing credentials:** no public EcoVadis medal, CDP score, NHS Evergreen level or UK CRP for Merit was found **[NOT FOUND]**.

## 3. What information customers want, and when

| Moment | What gets asked | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier onboarding / GPO contract set-up | Supplier code acknowledgement, enterprise ESG data, Scope 1/2 disclosure, emissions-reduction plan | Vizient, Premier, HealthTrust |
| Product contracting (per SKU) | EP attributes: PVC/DEHP/BPA/flame retardants/latex, recycled/recyclable packaging, reprocessability, end-of-life | Vizient (MindClick), Premier, Providence, Intermountain, Practice Greenhealth questions |
| Tender selection stage (UK) | Published CRP (pass/fail); Evergreen submission (Level 1 from 2026); modern-slavery questions (MSAT) | NHS England / NHS Supply Chain |
| Tender award stage | ≥10% net zero + social value score (UK); environmental criterion (France) | NHS; French public hospitals |
| Contract performance | Emissions-target and progress-reporting clauses; environmental performance clauses (France) | Kaiser, Vizient, French buyers |
| Annual / strategic-supplier engagement | Science-aligned targets (top vendors), CDP-style disclosure, progress updates | CommonSpirit top-30, Kaiser, SMI pharma CPOs |
| Coming next (2027–28) | Global Scope 3 CRP (NHS, Apr 2027); SBTi targets (pharma buyers, 2027); product carbon footprints (NHS TBD; pharma top-10 by 2028) | NHS, SMI members |

## 4. Implications for Merit

1. **The hard deadline is the UK, April 2027 (about 5 months after the competition).** Any NHS contract ≥£5M/yr or new framework will need a *published, global* Scope 1–3 CRP with a net-zero-by-2050 commitment, plus a current Evergreen assessment (Level 2 ≈ the 2027 standard). Merit publishes **no Scope 3 figure** today; its 2024 Scope 3 baseline is "in process" (outside-research §1.4). Supplier data is the main input to that Scope 3 number, which makes supplier data collection a revenue-protection item and not only a CSR item. Suggested sequence: finish the Scope 3 baseline using EcoVadis/supplier primary data for the top 78 suppliers (~65% of spend), publish a CRP, complete Evergreen, and aim for Level 2 by Q1 2027.
2. **In the US, frame it as "make the GPO's job easy", not "win on ESG".** Vizient, Premier and HealthTrust collect data but publish no weights, and US leaders rank reliability first. Pre-load Vizient/MindClick and Premier with complete EP attributes for every contracted SKU. Disclose Scope 1/2 plus the reduction plan they already ask for. Pitch supplier transparency as *supply resilience* (dual sourcing, country-risk mapping, labor due diligence). That plays to the #1 buying criterion and to the 2024 Baxter/Helene shortage memory.
3. **Chemicals are where hospitals ask most specifically, and Merit makes tubing, catheters and kits.** Providence and Intermountain name PVC and DEHP, Premier asks about bisphenols, flame retardants and heavy metals, and Kaiser's scorecard tracks chemicals of concern. Supplier material disclosures (resin, plasticizer, BPA content) are the supplier data with the most direct customer pull. Ask suppliers for them in the same EcoVadis/questionnaire cycle.
4. **CommonSpirit and Kaiser ask *their* top vendors for science-aligned targets.** If Merit sits in a system's top-30 emitters list, it will be asked for SBTi-style targets. Merit's 40%-by-2030 Scope 1+2 goal is not SBTi-validated and is at −2% vs 2020 (outside-research §1.4). Being able to show supplier engagement data is the credible Scope 3 story to bring to those strategic-account reviews.
5. **France already scores this.** Since Aug 21, 2026, every French public-hospital tender must include an environmental award criterion and an enforceable environmental clause. Merit has a French plant and EU sales. Packaging reduction, local manufacturing and documented supplier standards are easy points to score.
6. **OEM customers (pharma/medtech) are the strictest.** SMI pharma CPOs want SBTi targets by 2027, product footprints for top products by 2028, and the targets "integrated into contracting for your suppliers". Merit's OEM business ($205M in 2025) will see these flow-down asks first, and Merit then has to pass them on to its own suppliers.
7. **Join the pre-competitive forum.** CHARME's Sustainable Procurement workstream (Medtronic, BD, Boston Scientific, Stryker, Baxter, Philips, Kaiser, Vizient) is where US supplier expectations are being set. Its 2026 pilots (packaging optimization, order consolidation, modal shift off air freight) fit a disposables maker. Joining puts Merit in front of Vizient and Kaiser buyers on a cooperative footing.
8. **Make one data pack for all of them.** The same core set answers nearly every request: Scope 1/2/3 with method, targets, CRP, EcoVadis scorecard (or Vitals), supplier-code coverage, % of spend assessed, modern-slavery due-diligence results, and SKU-level chemicals and packaging. Package it once ("Merit Supplier Transparency Report" plus a customer portal or a share-on-request EcoVadis scorecard) and hand it to GPO contract managers at bid time and to NHS bids at the PSQ stage. **Disclosure to hold back until verified:** product-level carbon claims, which customers are only now standardizing (NHS method TBD; SMI LCA standard pending) and which carry greenwashing risk (outside-research §7).
