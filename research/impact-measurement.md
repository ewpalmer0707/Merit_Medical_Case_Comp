# Impact measurement: moving from completion rates to real outcomes for supplier workers, suppliers and patients

Compiled 2026-10-27 for the Merit Medical case (presenting Fri Oct 30). Every figure has a URL. **[UNVERIFIED]** = a secondary source, or a number that conflicts across sources. **[NOT FOUND]** = searched for, not located. **[assumption]** = our own estimate. **[my calc]** = arithmetic on sourced numbers. This file does not repeat what is already in `analogues-buyer-programs.md`, `analogues-mexico-china.md` or `outside-research.md` §5 (the Ballard Social Impact Cycle text and the outputs/outcomes/impact definitions). It adds to them.

**The problem in one line.** Our current scorecard (invited, completed Vitals, EcoVadis score ≥45) measures *outputs*: things Merit does and forms suppliers fill in. The Ballard Guidebook says outputs "measure activity, not effectiveness." The judges want *outcomes* (what changes for a worker or a plant) and *impact* (how much of that change Merit caused). See outside-research.md §5.1.

**Workforce turnover is the most practical worker outcome for Tijuana.**
- Tijuana maquila turnover has historically run **~10% per month**, and **13% per month** at one medical-products plant (Garcia & Rivas, about 2007; old). https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/395/39512458005.pdf
- Tijuana EDC (Oct 2025): the sector "typically sees turnover rates around 7–9%," period not stated **[UNVERIFIED unit]**. https://tijuanaedc.org/retention-strategies-in-tijuanas-nearshoring-success/
- **Merit's own Tijuana plant reported turnover of "less than 1%"** (Merit's managing director for Latin America, Oct 2016, with 450 employees then; period not stated). https://tijuanaedc.org/attracting-retaining-right-workforce-mexico/ . **Story angle:** Merit knows how to keep workers in Tijuana, and coaching can pass that practice to suppliers. Turnover is easy to measure (IMSS registrations and payroll), matters to workers, and predicts quality and continuity.
- **The 2027–2030 hours shock.** Mexico's constitutional reform (published DOF Mar 3, 2026) lowers the maximum week from 48 h (2026) to **46 (2027), 44 (2028), 42 (2029) and 40 (2030)**, with no pay cut. Overtime counts from hour 41, with a limit of 4 triple-paid hours. https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/mexico-officially-enacts-constitutional-reform ; https://mexicobusiness.news/talent/news/congress-clears-gradual-40-hour-workweek?tag=talent . **Cost to SMEs: [NOT FOUND].** This gives a ready-made, dated, legally defined worker outcome: *% of workers at Tier-2 suppliers within the legal weekly maximum for that year, with weekly pay held flat.*

## 3. Patient-level linkage: continuity of supply

The causal chain we can defend: healthier, better-run, better-financed critical suppliers → fewer supplier-caused interruptions → fewer Merit backorders → fewer cancelled or delayed procedures. (Shortage cost and harm data are already in outside-research.md §9: Premier ~$3.5M/yr per 650-bed system, 39% cancel procedures quarterly, and FDA lists angiographic injector/syringe (DXT) in shortage.)

What a device company can track:
- **Supplier-caused backorder lines and days** for products that rely on Tier-2 suppliers. Internal ERP data **[assumption]**.
- **Single-source exposure covered**: % of single-source parts whose supplier has a business-continuity plan and a qualified second source or safety stock.
- **FDA 506J notifications**. Device makers must notify FDA of a permanent discontinuance or interruption likely to cause meaningful disruption, during or before a public health emergency. Since the FY2023 Omnibus (506J(h)) they may notify voluntarily at any time. Final guidance Jan 6, 2025. Target: **zero 506J notifications caused by a Tier-2 supplier**. https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-supply-chain-and-shortages/supply-and-shortages-medical-devices-frequently-asked-questions ; https://www.alston.com/en/insights/publications/2025/01/fda-guidance-medical-device-shortages
- **HIRC Resiliency Badge** (Healthcare Industry Resilience Collaborative). An evidence-based assessment of manufacturers' demand planning, inventory, visibility, risk management and contingency, by product category. **40+ health systems** have pledged to recognize it. Medline holds Diamond; Baxter holds Gold (IV solutions); Stryker and BD hold Transparency badges. **A public patient-continuity metric that hospital customers already reward.** Specific scoring thresholds: **[NOT FOUND]**. https://beckershospitalreview.com/supply-chain/40-systems-promote-resiliency-badge-for-suppliers.html ; https://www.hpnonline.com/sourcing-logistics/news/53098409/four-dozen-provider-orgs-pledge-to-adopt-hirc-resiliency-badge ; https://www.hpnonline.com/sourcing-logistics/news/55135118/medline-earns-diamond-level-healthcare-industry-resilience-collaborative-resiliency-badge ; https://www.stryker.com/us/en/about/news/2023/stryker-awarded-transparency-partner-distinction-by-the-healthca.html . Whether Merit holds a HIRC badge: **[NOT FOUND]**.

## 4. Frameworks

### 4.1 Theory of change / logic model for the Merit Supplier Ladder
The structure follows the Ballard Guidebook definitions (outputs → outcomes → impact; outside-research.md §5.1). Each link is labelled with the evidence behind it.

```
INPUTS            Coordinator (2 FTE), subsidized EcoVadis seats, coach hours, NAFIN factoring line, IFC PDP chapter
ACTIVITIES        Pre-filled baseline -> Vitals -> coaching visit (energy meter, hours plan, grievance channel) -> CAP -> EcoVadis
OUTPUTS           % invited / completed / coached / CAPs closed         <- what we track today (activity, not effect)
SHORT OUTCOMES    Supplier: has hours plan for 46h (2027), energy baseline, working grievance channel; days-to-close CAP falls
(yr 1)            Worker: knows and trusts the channel (survey); hours within legal max
MEDIUM OUTCOMES   Worker: turnover down, recordable injuries down, harassment concern down, weekly pay held while hours fall
(yr 2-3)          Supplier: energy cost/unit down, defect PPM down, cheaper working capital, new customers via shared scorecard
LONG-TERM IMPACT  Supplier stays a Merit supplier (retention); zero supplier-caused backorders/506J -> patients get procedures on time
ASSUMPTIONS       Better conditions -> lower turnover -> better quality (Better Work +22% productivity; 4pp more orders, contested)
                  Help + carrot beats mandate (CDP/HSBC 52%, already sourced); coaching transfers capability (TSSC, Nike lean, but not China)
```

### 4.2 Standard indicator sets to borrow (so our metrics are not invented)
- **ILO Decent Work Indicators** (manual, 2nd ed. 2013): 75 statistical + 21 legal indicators in 10 elements. Use **"employment in excessive working time (>48 h/week)"**, the low-pay rate, occupational injury rates, and stability (tenure/turnover). https://www.ituc-csi.org/IMG/pdf/ILO_DW_indicators.pdf ; https://ilostat.ilo.org/measuring-job-quality-difficult-but-necessary/
- **IRIS+ (GIIN)**: OI4724 *Employees earning a living wage or higher* (designed for one's own staff but can be applied to a supplier's staff); PI1492 *payments to supplier individuals*; OI3547 *hires*. **IRIS+ has no ready-made supply-chain worker-wellbeing or turnover metric [NOT FOUND]**. https://irisdev.thegiin.org/metric/5.3/oi4724 ; https://irisdev.thegiin.org/metric/5.3/pi1492 ; https://irisdev.thegiin.org/metric/5.3/oi3547
- **Nike EWB survey** is published as an open industry-standard instrument through Labor Solutions, so Merit can reuse proven questions instead of writing its own. https://www.laborsolutions.tech/post/nike-s-engagement-and-wellbeing-survey-now-available-to-anyone

### 4.3 How to set baselines cheaply
1. **Data that already exists at Merit**: spend, PPM, OTIF, lead time, backorders, price requests. Cost $0.
2. **Data the supplier already has to keep by Mexican law**: IMSS-registered headcount and wage base (turnover and pay), STPS injury records, **NOM-035 survey results**, CFE electricity bills (energy cost per unit). Ask for these on day 1 of coaching. Cost ≈ coach time.
3. **One worker-voice pulse** at baseline and again at month 12 (EcoVadis Worker Voice, since Merit is already a client). Budget **~$40–75 per respondent [my calc from 60 Decibels]** × ~100 per site × 15 sites ≈ **$60–110k per wave [assumption]**. A cheaper option is SMS-only (Laborlink-style), with an expected response rate of 30–40%.
4. **Attribution without an expensive study**: copy the IFC Mexico SDP design. Pilot cohort (15 Tijuana suppliers, 2026–27) against the **wait-listed** Tier-2 cohort that starts in 2028. Compare the change in turnover, PPM and OTIF (difference-in-differences, the same method used in the Chile SDP evaluation). This lets us claim **impact** (the share Merit caused), not just outcomes, which is the Ballard Guidebook's own test.
