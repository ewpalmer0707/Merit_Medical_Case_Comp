# Buyer-company analogues: programs for small, low-readiness, critical suppliers

**Purpose.** Real-world cases of buyers that faced Merit Medical's problem (critical small suppliers that cannot or will not meet ESG data demands) and what they did about it. Compiled 2026-10-24 from public sources only. Every fact carries its URL. Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from a secondary or vendor source I could not confirm against a primary document; **[NOT FOUND]** means I searched and could not locate the item.

**Merit context used for relevance.** ~1,030 direct suppliers in 42 countries; the hard group is small, single-plant, sole-source or hard-to-replace component makers (many in Mexico and China) with no sustainability staff, no emissions tracking, sometimes no English documentation, and stalled EcoVadis assessments. Requalifying a supplier takes 9–12 months, so dropping them is not a real option.

## 2. Case detail

### 2.1 Medtronic (closest peer)

- **Problem.** Medtronic buys from 68,162 suppliers in 138 countries. Its supplier sustainability program launched FY19, focused on top-spend, inherently high-risk suppliers and contract manufacturers. Most gaps found were "missing policies and best practices," i.e. suppliers that had never written anything down, not suppliers doing harm. https://www.3blmedia.com/news/putting-purpose-action-supply-chain-responsibility
- **What they asked.** A self-assessment questionnaire sets a baseline; Medtronic scores risk and sets the re-assessment cadence by tier (high risk every year, medium every 2 years, low every 3). On-site audits only for suppliers rated high-risk two years running (deferred during COVID, virtual option planned). https://www.3blmedia.com/news/putting-purpose-action-supply-chain-responsibility
- **What suppliers got.** One-on-one training sessions with "a significant number of suppliers," plus sample policies suppliers could adapt. 116 high/medium-risk suppliers received "improvement request forms." https://www.3blmedia.com/news/putting-purpose-action-supply-chain-responsibility
- **Decarbonization layer (Oct 2025).** The Supplier Decarbonization Program is "developed with modularity in mind… meeting suppliers where they're at," with four steps: Calculate → Set targets → Report annually (CDP or Watershed) → Implement. Support = guides on the Supplier Essentials site, a dedicated Responsible Supply Management contact channel, and ongoing webinars/forums. Target: 75% of suppliers by emissions have science-based targets by 2030; net zero by 2045. https://www.medtronic.com/content/dam/medtronic-wide/public/united-states/customer-support-services/distributor-resources/supplier-decarbonization-executive-summary.pdf
- **Results.** 320 suppliers completed the questionnaire in the first two years; 34 improved their risk rating in FY20. In FY22, suppliers representing 66% of managed spend had been assessed vs. an 80% FY26 target. https://www.3blmedia.com/news/putting-purpose-action-supply-chain-responsibility ; https://news.medtronic.com/2022-12-14-Medtronic-named-as-a-leading-sustainability-company
- **Gaps.** No published EcoVadis link, no cost figures, no breakdown of small or Mexican/Chinese suppliers. [NOT FOUND]
- **Lesson for Merit.** A direct medtech peer treats "no policy" as a training problem, not a compliance failure: it hands suppliers templates and a human, then re-tests on a risk-based clock. Merit can quote this as proof that a modular, maturity-based ask is already the medtech norm.

### 2.2 Philips (medtech; "beyond auditing")

- **Problem.** Philips concluded that audit-based compliance "did little to drive lasting improvement," created audit fatigue, and encouraged suppliers to hide problems. Historic data point: only 57 of 410 Chinese suppliers (14%) were in its earlier health-and-safety program. https://www.philips.com/c-dam/corporate/about-philips/sustainability/downloads/ecovision-methodologies/Supplier-Sustainability-Goals-and-Progress-2016.pdf ; https://www.philips.com/a-w/about/news/archive/features/2023/20231026-supply-chain-sustainability-teaming-up-with-suppliers-to-protect-people-and-the-planet.html
- **What they asked.** Annual self-assessment validated by Philips experts; suppliers sorted into four buckets (Best in Class, Supplier Sustainability Improvement Plan, Do-It-Yourself, Potential Zero Tolerance). Only the improvement-plan bucket gets deep-dive assessments and customized support; BiC suppliers just re-self-assess. Machine learning predicts maturity to set engagement intensity. https://www.philips.com/a-w/about/environmental-social-governance/environmental/supplier-sustainability/supplier-sustainability-performance
- **What suppliers got.** Expert problem-solving, help writing action plans and timetables, online and offline meetings, performance reviews and data analysis. Graduates (e.g., Xiamen Salom) run a Tier-2 version on their own suppliers. https://www.philips.com/a-w/about/news/archive/features/2023/20231026-supply-chain-sustainability-teaming-up-with-suppliers-to-protect-people-and-the-planet.html
- **Results.** Haishun Electric (China, joined 2020): 120+ improvement areas jointly identified; projected 350,000 kWh/yr energy and 9,000 t/yr water saved; tenfold wastewater-quality improvement. "Hundreds of suppliers" participate yearly. Philips target: 50% of spend committed to science-based targets by 2025. https://www.philips.com/a-w/about/news/archive/standard/news/articles/2021/20211208-philips-sustainability-program-puts-collaboration-ahead-of-auditing-to-reduce-supply-chain-co2-emissions.html
- **Gaps.** Program cost and participant counts not published. [NOT FOUND]
- **Lesson for Merit.** A medtech buyer explicitly replaced audits with coached improvement plans for Chinese suppliers and reported concrete plant-level savings. The four-bucket model is a ready template for sorting Merit's 1,030 suppliers so that only the stuck ones get intensive help.

### 2.3 Johnson & Johnson

- **What they asked.** Suppliers in the Supplier Sustainability Program (generally >80% of global cross-category spend plus high-risk/high-impact suppliers) must complete EcoVadis; highest GHG emitters also disclose to CDP. Low scorers re-assess within a year; higher scorers every 2–3 years. EcoVadis results decide who gets an on-site audit. https://healthforhumanityreport.jnj.com/2023/accountability-innovation/responsible-supply-base/supplier-sustainability.html ; https://www.jnj.com/suppliers/sustainability
- **What suppliers got.** "Onward" upskilling: one-hour webinar sessions on human rights, climate emissions, etc. (free; attendance numbers [NOT FOUND]). EH&S technical visits. https://www.jnj.com/suppliers/onward-and-upward-procurements-training-program-prepares-suppliers-to-advance-johnson-johnsons-sustainability-priorities
- **Results.** 989 suppliers completed EcoVadis in 2023; 150+ EH&S audits and technical visits in 2023; 85% of invited suppliers responded to CDP climate and 74% to water in 2022 (six consecutive years on the CDP Supply Chain Leaderboard). https://healthforhumanityreport.jnj.com/2022/accountability-innovation/responsible-supply-base/supplier-engagement.html
- **Lesson for Merit.** Spend-based EcoVadis coverage plus risk-based re-assessment keeps the assessment burden off the long tail. Pair a formal rating with a lightweight free webinar series so small suppliers learn the vocabulary before they are scored.

### 2.4 GSK (pharma; buyer-paid platform for small suppliers)

- **Problem / design.** Program launched Sept 2022 "to democratize sustainability by sharing standards, training, and learnings." Manufacture 2030 (now Secaro) targets SME suppliers with a measurement platform; GSK "fully sponsored membership fees for the first 200 API supplier sites" joining Activate. Energize (with other pharma companies) gives suppliers without in-house expertise access to aggregated renewable-electricity contracting. https://www.dcatvci.org/features/executive-one-on-one-gsks-sustainable-procurement-program-for-suppliers/
- **Results.** 160+ critical suppliers in initial phases; Energize 210 registered suppliers, first cohorts working toward 2 TWh. https://www.dcatvci.org/features/executive-one-on-one-gsks-sustainable-procurement-program-for-suppliers/
- **Lesson for Merit.** Buyer-paid platform seats for the critical few is a proven pharma move. Merit could sponsor EcoVadis/Vitals or a carbon-measurement seat for its sole-source tail.

### 2.5 AstraZeneca (pharma; explicit spend tiering)

- Rules: EcoVadis mandatory only above USD 250k/yr spend (minimum score 45, first-timers allowed to improve progressively; 6–8 week assessment, up to 3 months re-assessment); CDP only above USD 1.3m/yr (no fee when buyer-requested); everyone else only registers on the free Worldfavor tool. AZ does **not** reimburse EcoVadis fees. Support = Activate (Secaro), Energize, PSCI, Clean Heat program, scheduled "Committed-to-Advanced" EcoVadis webinars, Pharma LCA tool sessions. https://www.astrazeneca.com/partnering/supplier-sustainability-hub.html
- EcoVadis gave AZ its 2024 Sustainability Achievement Award for supplier engagement. https://resources.ecovadis.com/news-press/ecovadis-announces-2024-sustainability-achievement-awards
- **Lesson for Merit.** Publish spend thresholds so a $60k/yr sole-source machinist knows up front that they only need the free tier.

### 2.6 PSCI (Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Initiative; Merit-adjacent consortium)

- 81 members representing 70%+ of industry revenue. "One audit, many customers": PSCI audits are shared across members so small Indian/Chinese suppliers are not audited repeatedly. https://pscinitiative.org/home
- Capability building: annual supplier conferences in India and China (2,100+ supplier attendees in 2022); free resource library; webinars in Mandarin (Decarbonisation Playbook walkthrough, renewable energy sourcing in China) and Japanese; PSCI Maturity Model app; Principles e-learning. 2024: 1,208 audits on platform, 1,100 webinar attendees, 2,100 registered supplier sites; 2025 report cites "record audit sharing and growth in Asia." https://pscinitiative.org/resources?audience=3 ; https://pscinitiative.org/supportingSuppliers
- BSI case: a European pharma company piloted 3 audits then scaled to 7 countries focused on India and China. https://www.bsigroup.com/globalassets/localfiles/en-gb/healthcare/supply-chain/bsi-psci-healthcare-a4-flyer.pdf
- **Lesson for Merit.** Medtech lacks an equivalent of PSCI/TfS/RBA. Merit can either join an existing scheme (RBA has medtech members; PSCI accepts healthcare companies) or propose the shared-audit, local-language-conference model to peers so its Mexican/Chinese suppliers face one ask, not ten.

### 2.7 Schneider Electric Zero Carbon Project

- **Problem.** ~75% of its top-1,000 suppliers had never quantified carbon emissions. https://procurementleaders.com/content/schneider-electric-sustainabile-procurement-strategy-net-zero/
- **Design.** Carbon-maturity survey via the SRM platform sorts suppliers into Aspiring / Advanced / Leaders, with simplified communication for early-stage firms. Initial emissions estimated from spend and category factors so suppliers start from a number rather than a blank. Scope 3 not required. Three steps: baseline → goal → action plan. https://procurementleaders.com/content/schneider-electric-sustainabile-procurement-strategy-net-zero/
- **What suppliers got.** 8 virtual technical courses (~3 hours each) in multiple languages and time zones, 40 technical sessions, 50+ one-on-one sessions, monthly peer forum, dedicated web portal, Zeigo Network platform, access to PPA counterparties. Built by 17 internal experts. https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/sustainability/zero-carbon-project ; https://procurementleaders.com/content/schneider-electric-sustainabile-procurement-strategy-net-zero/
- **Results.** 1,000+ suppliers joined (70% of upstream emissions); 1,300+ participants trained; by end-2021, 10% of suppliers had cut emissions 5%; company reports 53% (Q3 2025) to 56% (end-2025) average Scope 1+2 reduction vs. 50% target. Next phase onboarding 1,100+ suppliers. https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/sustainability/zero-carbon-project ; https://tedmag.com/schneider-electric-updates-q3-2025-sustainability-impact-report/ ; https://esgpost.com/schneider-electric-cuts-supplier-emissions-56-surpasses-800-mt-co₂-target/ [company-reported, not independently audited]
- **Lesson for Merit.** Segment by maturity, pre-fill the first estimate from spend data, and run training in the supplier's language and time zone. Three-hour modules, not a 100-question form.

## 4. Academic and consultancy evidence on SME supplier ESG engagement

**Lower-tier and small suppliers are the riskiest and the least engaged.**
- Villena & Gioia (JOM 2018; HBR Mar–Apr 2020) studied 3 MNCs, 9 tier-1 and 22 lower-tier suppliers: lower-tier suppliers "address their environmental and labor issues passively and constitute the riskiest suppliers." Remedies: a central coordinator in the MNC plus direct, indirect (via tier-1), collective (industry) and global (NGO/standards) approaches. https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/on-the-riskiness-of-lower-tier-suppliers-managing-sustainability- ; https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Veronica-Villena/publication/339486951_A_More_Sustainable_Supply_Chain/links/61869b4a3068c54fa5b5d59e/A-More-Sustainable-Supply-Chain.pdf
- CDP: only 22% (2017) and 23% (2018) of responding suppliers engaged their own suppliers; barriers cited: no experience calculating emissions, no leverage, program cost, no mandate. https://www.carbontrust.com/news-and-insights/news/big-buyers-harness-the-power-of-purchasing-to-deliver-emissions-reductions ; https://www.reutersevents.com/sustainability/node/56078

**Questionnaire design and assessment fatigue (strongest empirical study found).**
- Fraser, Müller & Schwarzkopf (2020), 23,473 responses to an automotive OEM's internal questionnaire and 5,431 of 8,093 (67%) completions of an industry-wide SAQ from 70 countries. Internal yes/no form averaged 89% with ~40% perfect scores (useless); evidence-backed industry SAQ averaged 65% with real variance. Over 250 suppliers accidentally answered "yes" to using involuntary labour, evidence of tick-box rushing. Suppliers welcomed one shared SAQ instead of many. Mexico and China suppliers scored 73% on the SAQ vs Germany 62%, partly self-selection. Recommendations: standardise across the industry, require evidence, avoid leading questions, give automated improvement feedback, vary questions by supplier type. Required certifications (ISO 14001) are "costly and burdensome for smaller suppliers." https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7662027/
- Maplecroft: most SAQs exceed 100 questions; uncoordinated multi-buyer requests cause fatigue, skipped questions, low response; recommends risk-tiered question sets. https://maplecroft.com/insights/analysis/why-do-we-need-smart-supplier-self-assessment-questionnaires
- US EPA Center for Corporate Climate Leadership flags "survey fatigue" as a known supplier issue. https://www.assent.com/?p=40452

**SME capacity and the "pushing out small suppliers" harm.**

- BDC Canada (121 large buyers, 1,251 SME suppliers, fall 2022): 82% of major buyers require at least one ESG disclosure, expected 92% in 2024; 75% plan to add criteria within 5 years; SMEs cite new business (50%), recruitment (32%), financing (31%) as benefits. https://www.bdc.ca/en/about/mediaroom/news-releases/small-businesses-left-out-big-contracts-without-esg-reporting-bdc-study

## 5. Patterns that worked / patterns that failed

**Worked**
1. **Tier the ask by maturity or spend, and say so publicly.** Philips (4 buckets), Schneider (Aspiring/Advanced/Leaders), AstraZeneca ($250k / $1.3m thresholds), Medtronic (risk-based re-assessment clock), Bosch (CDP SME form). Small suppliers get the light form; intensive help goes only to the stuck-but-critical.
2. **Pre-fill the first number.** Schneider estimated baselines from spend so suppliers corrected rather than created. Nike and Philips sent engineers to identify the first 100 fixes.
3. **Buyer pays for the tool, supplier pays a token.** GSK sponsored 200 platform seats; HSBC/EcoVadis Mexico subsidises but does not zero the fee ("paying demonstrates value… higher response rates"); CDP disclosure is free when the buyer requests it.
4. **Train in the supplier's language and time zone.** Schneider multi-language 3-hour modules; PSCI Mandarin webinars and China/India conferences; UNGC Spain Spanish/Portuguese course; HP offline mobile training; EcoVadis Vitals in 14 languages.
5. **One shared assessment instead of ten.** PSCI, TfS, RBA, Sedex, and the industry SAQ in the Fraser study all cut fatigue; suppliers in the study were "glad to complete one shared SAQ."
6. **Make the carrot financial and near-term.** Walmart/HSBC cheaper early payment by CDP score; Bosch bonus/malus and supplier award; Herdez/BBVA preferential factoring in Mexico; Walmart's Gigaton PPA giving small suppliers big-buyer renewable pricing.
7. **Recognition ladders beat pass/fail.** Walmart Giga Guru, GM phased minimum score of 50 (average landed at 52), AstraZeneca "progressive improvement" toward 45.
8. **Send a person.** Medtronic one-on-ones with sample policies (34 suppliers improved ratings in a year), Toyota TSSC floor coaching, Continental's two-year seminar series, Philips expert visits.

**Failed or under-delivered**
1. **Audit-only compliance.** Philips abandoned it: audit fatigue, hidden problems, no lasting improvement. Villena & Gioia found lower-tier suppliers remain passive under monitor-only regimes.
2. **Unverified yes/no questionnaires.** The OEM's internal form averaged 89% with 40% perfect scores and 250+ suppliers accidentally confessing to forced labour; data was unusable.
3. **Mandate without support.** Make UK: 77% of SME manufacturers get ESG demands, fewer than half have resources, ~27% get help; 56% of large Finnish firms have already swapped suppliers, i.e. the attrition harm is real.
4. **Fee-on-supplier with no return.** SME Climate Hub: 84% offered no incentive; WEF/ITC: producers shoulder most cost. Santander-style "supplier covers EcoVadis fully" rules work for large suppliers but are the exact failure mode for Merit's tail.
5. **Expecting fast conversion.** Supplier LoCT: ~18% SBT commitment after multi-year coaching; Schneider: only 10% of suppliers had cut 5% after year one; UNGC Spain: ~40% course completion. Plan on multi-year horizons and partial uptake.
6. **Skipping the coordinator role.** HBR: goals alone fall short without a named internal owner who disseminates practices across the network.
