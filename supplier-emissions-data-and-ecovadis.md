# Supplier emissions data, EcoVadis, and Mexican reporting

Research synthesized October 27, 2026. Read with the [current proposal](../ideas/supplier-reporting-support.md). Legal applicability must be checked for each entity and reporting year.

## What Merit actually needs

Supplier Scope 1/2 figures are inputs to Merit's purchased-goods Scope 3 calculations, not a complete product footprint. Do not add every supplier's entire footprint to Merit. Account for products purchased, defensible allocation, relevant upstream materials/production, and gaps filled with secondary data. Other Scope 3 categories need separate assessment.

[GHG Protocol Category 1 guidance](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards_supporting/Chapter1.pdf) permits spend-based, average-data, supplier-specific, and hybrid methods. Spend or physical quantities can support initial estimates before a supplier has a complete inventory.

## Minimum to begin, versus evidence to improve

**Merit may already hold:** annual spend by supplier/product category, reporting period, and purchasing/shipment records. Document factor selection, currency/price-year treatment where relevant, calculation boundaries, and limitations.

**Small initial supplier request:**
- Legal identity, manufacturing site(s), reporting period, named contact.
- Product descriptions and quantities supplied, with units.
- Material type and weight where available.
- Existing reports/assessments and permission to share.
- Source references, confirmation of figures, and disclosed gaps.

This can support an initial estimate. It does not by itself establish complete supplier-specific or independently verified data.

**Improve with:** annual electricity and fuel records; refrigerant/process-source screening; production records; factor references; supported allocation; upstream materials and other relevant stages. Preserve estimates for missing stages and avoid adding a factory calculation on top of an average factor that already includes it.

A robust pack has boundaries, records, factors/units, calculations, estimates/exclusions, allocation, review findings, and supplier sign-off. Independent assurance is a further, separately described evidence level.

## Accounting lessons carried forward

- Activity data (e.g., kWh) multiplied by a compatible emission factor yields emissions for that source.
- CO2e expresses the warming effect of different greenhouse gases on a common scale.
- Equal product counts support allocation only when they reasonably represent production effort. Machine hours can help; power differences and shared electricity still matter.
- Total emissions can stay unchanged while emissions per product fall. Merit's assigned contribution may then fall for unchanged purchases, subject to consistent boundaries and allocation.
- Use factors appropriate to the geography, supply, method, and reporting period. Keep the source and version.
- Missing data is not zero. Primary supplier records and secondary estimates should be distinguished.
- A traceable calculation is checkable; it is not automatically independently verified.

Sources: [GHG Protocol collection guidance](https://ghgptechassistance.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/37993461124244-How-do-I-collect-primary-data-from-suppliers-and-other-value-chain-partners), [factor guidance](https://ghgprotocol.org/calculation-tools-faq).

## EcoVadis: assessment and quantitative data are separate needs

EcoVadis Ratings covers environment, labor/human rights, ethics, and sustainable procurement. A score does not replace an emissions dataset. Existing CDP/SMETA/other reports may provide evidence, but are not interchangeable EcoVadis ratings.

[Carbon Action Manager](https://support.ecovadis.com/hc/en-us/articles/17031070956562-What-is-the-Carbon-Action-Manager) describes supplier emissions metrics, reliability levels, and product-data sharing. Confirm Merit's subscription, exports, supplier eligibility, permissions, and access to evidence.

On [supplier plans](https://ecovadis.com/plans-pricing/), Basic supports broad assessment and in-network sharing; Carbon Rating is a focused invitation-based route. Corporate is for group entities, not automatically independent suppliers. Academy access is listed after assessment: do not rely on it for first-submission support. Pricing varies and must be quoted.

[Accredited consultants](https://support.ecovadis.com/hc/en-us/articles/8515575617426-EcoVadis-Accredited-Consulting-Partners) may assist with assessment preparation. Suppliers must register themselves and remain involved. Confirm authorized access before Merit edits/submits supplier responses.

[Evidence rules](https://support.ecovadis.com/hc/en-us/articles/210460307-Understanding-supporting-documents) require genuine company documentation; copied or questionnaire-only paperwork is not a substitute for implemented practices.

## Mexican reporting: applicability first

| Route | Relevance | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| SEMARNAT RENE | Environmental inventory/filing; useful emissions evidence | Covered activity, reporting boundary, and threshold tests apply; small size alone is not the test |
| NIF/NIS | Domestic financial-reporting sustainability disclosures, including IBSO | Determine whether supplier reports under Mexican NIF; emissions alone is not the full NIS disclosure |
| CNBV / IFRS S1/S2 | Issuer sustainability-related financial disclosures | Applies to in-scope issuers; financial entities have separate provisions |

[SEMARNAT](https://www.gob.mx/semarnat/acciones-y-programas/registro-nacional-de-emisiones-rene?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block) provides RENE resources; [CINIF](https://cinif.org.mx/exclusivo_guias.php) provides NIS guidance. CINIF is an independent standard setter, not government.

The [CNBV January 2025 rule](https://www.dof.gob.mx/abrirPDF.php?anio=2025&archivo=28012025-MAT.pdf&repo=repositorio%2F) establishes issuer reporting from 2026 for FY2025, with assurance phased in. Do not assign all suppliers all three reports. None automatically satisfies EcoVadis.

## Government and provider leads

- [CINIF free calculator notice](https://www.cinif.org.mx/uploads/2026-Aviso_Mejoras_herramientas_NIS.pdf): reporting resource, not a government grant.
- [Baja California certification reimbursement](https://www.bajacalifornia.gob.mx/coordinaciondegabinete/Prensa/NoticiaCompleta/27162): conditional certification support; EcoVadis/coaching eligibility and remaining funds unconfirmed.
- [IFC project](https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/AS/609771/supplier-development-program-in-mexico): automotive focus; Merit/medical-device access unconfirmed.
- [KPMG Mexico](https://kpmg.com/mx/es/servicios/asesoria/administracion-de-riesgos/servicios-de-asesoria-contable/reportes-financieros-asg.html): NIS/IFRS reporting and customized training expertise.
- [SGS Mexico](https://www.sgs.com/es-mx/service-groups/formacion-en-sostenibilidad-y-ambiente-social-y-gobernanza-asg): GHG/ESG capability development.
- [Arizmendi](https://www.arizmendiconsultores.com/gestion-de-emisiones/): advertises inventories, RENE/COA and environmental services.

These are service leads, not endorsed vendors, confirmed partners, or quotes. For the current proposal, request individual reporting assistance rather than a regional training package.

## U.S. applicability caution

Selling to Merit does not automatically create a U.S. filing obligation for a Mexican supplier. [SEC's May 2026 announcement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-49-sec-proposes-rescission-climate-related-disclosure-rules) concerns proposed rescission of stayed rules, not proof of a completed repeal. [CARB resources](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/corporate-ghg-reporting/resources) should be checked for current coverage and rules. Confirm Merit's entity-specific duties before claiming compliance.
