# Targeted supplier support: budget, assumptions, and decisions

October 27, 2026. Companion to the [proposal](../ideas/supplier-reporting-support.md).

## What changed during the discussion

| Earlier idea | Current position |
|---|---|
| Regional kickoff and group training | Dropped; individual support only where needed |
| Train suppliers on all Mexican reporting routes | Check applicability and provide relevant assistance |
| Collect reports, then Merit fills EcoVadis for everyone | Reuse evidence; supplier retains ownership; confirm authorized assistance |

| Approximately $70k for each 10 suppliers | Not a validated unit cost; earlier cohort model included shared program costs |

This evolution applies to this proposal, not automatically to the team's consolidated strategy.

## How to build the current budget

Total = central setup + central review/coordination + sum of approved individual assistance + incremental platform/subscriptions + required verification/assurance + contingency.

Count supplier employee hours separately from Merit's cash spending. Reused staff capacity is not free, but loaded staff cost is not necessarily additional cash. Obtain quotes and avoid double-counting existing licenses or shared activities.

For each assistance request record the barrier, owner, deliverable, specialist hours/rate or fixed fee, deadline, cap, and acceptance test. Let a Procurement owner and Sustainability/data lead approve support. Site visits need a specific purpose.

## Previous model: context only, NOT current approval request

| Item | Illustrative USD |
|---|---:|
| Design/preparation: 60 hours x $150 | 9,000 |
| Group delivery/office hours: 40 hours x $150 | 6,000 |
| Coaching: 12 suppliers x 12 hours x $150 | 21,600 |
| Technical review: 12 suppliers x 5 hours x $150 | 9,000 |
| Event/travel/translation allowance | 5,000 |
| External subtotal | 50,600 |
| 20% contingency | 10,120 |
| External total | 60,720 |
| Merit staff: 200 hours x $60 | 12,000 |
| Total resource cost | 72,720 |

This modeled 12 suppliers over 12 months, not one supplier. Rates/hours were assumptions, not quotes. It excluded subscriptions, independent assurance, supplier employee time, and taxes. Regional/group items no longer belong in the current design; do not simply divide this model to price individual support.

## Financial and values fit

The case's CGI commitments are 5–7% organic constant-currency growth, 20–22% non-GAAP operating margin in 2026, and at least $400M cumulative FCF. Treat financial scale as a constraint, not evidence that reporting pays for itself.

The old $72,720 resource model divided by approximately $1.516B case revenue is about 0.0048 percentage points of margin, or 0.48 basis points, if all cost were incremental operating expense. This is a scale illustration, not ROI.

Current approach: cap initial spending; review cost per accepted pack; document recurring costs before expansion. Do not promise prevented recalls, worker improvements, new sales, or avoided shortages without evidence of the causal link.

## Open decisions before the final deck

- How many suppliers fall into each readiness/support category? No evidence yet supports 85% readiness among the top 78.
- What accepted data fields are required for each selected calculation method?
- Which suppliers merit intensive help, using both continuity and emissions relevance?
- What capacity can Merit staff provide, and who owns review and consolidation?
- What are individual-service quotes and platform/subscription charges?
- What initial cap and escalation authority should executives approve?
- Which commercial incentives are genuinely needed and authorized?
- Which filings apply to particular supplier entities?
- How will the global proposal address broader ESG and China while this workstream focuses on Mexican emissions data?

## Timing and acceptance

Proposed start: launch requests/readiness diagnosis in 2026 if feasible; completion can extend into 2027. For assisted suppliers, a first reviewed pack in 4–8 weeks after engagement and stronger reporting in 6–12 months are planning assumptions. Reliable annual records may take longer when historical data is absent.

Prior group-course examples such as [Loblaw's four-month program](https://www.loblaw.ca/en/carbon-fight/) support the need for ongoing help but are not completion-time benchmarks for this individual-support design.

An accepted pack needs a defined period/boundary, source references, documented method/factors, explained gaps, supported allocation where used, and supplier approval. Measure whether it improves or substantiates Merit's inventory, not merely whether a file arrived.
